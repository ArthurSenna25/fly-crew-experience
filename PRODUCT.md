# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective aviation students (aspiring flight attendants) seeking entry into elite aviation training. They are not shopping for a course — they are seeking transformation, confidence, and access to the aviation lifestyle. They want to feel they are joining an exclusive community, not enrolling in a technical class.

## Product Purpose

Transforming insecurity into professional presence; preparing professionals who don't just pass interviews but mark presence before speaking. The brand's Big Idea is: "Profissionais preparados não apenas passam em entrevistas. Eles marcam presença antes mesmo de falar." The emotional Brand Promise is: "Transformar insegurança em presença profissional."

## Brand Personality

Elegant, authoritative, transformative
(Also aligned with: prestigious, mentoring, aspirational)

## Anti-references

Budget flight school aesthetic; cheap/technical prep-course look; aggressive sales marketing; generic SaaS startup patterns (neon gradients, badges); cluttered or overly busy design; anything that feels desperate, juvenile, or overly casual. The brand must never look like a 'cursinho' or discount technical course.

## Design Principles

- Elegant, secure, refined, and welcoming
- Whispers rather than shouts — no exaggeration, no loud marketing
- Inspired, human, sophisticated without being cold
- Refined & Tonal elevation philosophy (consistent with "marcas premium sussurram, não gritam" — sombras pesadas/táteis tendem a contradizer essa filosofia de discrição)

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard web accessibility is sufficient (WCAG 2.1 AA compliance).

## Notes for DESIGN.md

- **Color Character** (already defined in brand manual):
  - Azul Midnight Premium: #0B1F33 (Authority, trust, aviation stability)
  - Gold Prestige: #D4AF37 (Excellence, achievement, exclusivity)
  - Cloud White: #F7F7F5 (Lightness, premium cleanliness, space)
  - Executive Black: #111111 (Strength, luxury contrast, refinement)
  - Silver Mist: #AEB7C1 (Modern balance, discreet sophistication)
  - Hierarchy: Midnight Premium and Gold Prestige are PRIMARY. Cloud White, Executive Black and Silver Mist are SECONDARY/supporting.

- **Typography**:
  - Cinzel — headlines institucionais, campanhas principais, títulos premium
  - Montserrat — corpo de texto, redes sociais, materiais digitais, website
  - Inter — interfaces, dashboards, PDFs (uso restrito a contexto funcional/sistema, NÃO para headlines ou marketing)

- **Logo**: escudo (prestígio, instituição, proteção, comunidade) + asas (ascensão, liberdade, evolução, carreira).

- **Creative North Star (metáfora)**: The Aviation Atelier
